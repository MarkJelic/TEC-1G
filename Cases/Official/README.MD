# Official TEC-1G Case

Designing a 3D printable case for the TC-1G is one of the most satisfying yet frustrating things I have ever done.
Being a perfectionist is a blessing and a curse, and working in 3D needs patience!

The finished product is beautiful with a number of features a plain Retex box can't give:

1. Pick your colour -  Match it to your PCB colour or go with a standard grey or beige.
2. Battery Pack compatible - In the back left corner there is the perfect slot for the Lithium battery pack, making the TEC-1G a truly portable computer.
3. Stylish Cutout - The TEC-1G logo is cut out on the back that not only allows ventilation but could also be "blinged up" with coloured LEDs.
4. Keyboard Pogo Connector - At the front there is a removable slot cover that allows the the use of a 20 pin magnetic Pogo connector to the Matric Keybaord.
5. Streamlined Keyboard - Integrates perfectly with the Matrix Keyboard which also has an official case.
