AIM: TO SAFELY LAND ON THE PLANET OF MAGRATHA

NOTE: IF THERE ARE TOO MANY ITEMS IN THE THING, DROP SOME TOOLS AT THE BRIDGE

EARTH (AS ARTHUR)
-----------------
TURN ON LIGHT,  STAND, GET GOWN, WEAR  GOWN, OPEN POCKET, TAKE  PILL [10], GET
ALL, S, GET MAIL,  S, LIE DOWN, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, "FORD,  WHAT ABOUT MY HOME",
WAIT,  S, W,  WAIT,  DRINK BEER  [5],  DRINK  BEER [5],  DRINK  BEER [5],  BUY
SANDWICH, E, FEED DOG SANDWICH, N, WAIT, WAIT, GET DEVICE, PRESS GREEN

VOGON SHIP
----------
WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, SMELL ,  EXAMINE SHADOW [8], EAT PEANUTS, REMOVE GOWN,
HANG  GOWN ON  HOOK, WAIT,  GET TOWEL  AND SATCHEL,  PUT TOWEL  ON GRATE,  PUT
SATCHEL IN FRONT  OF PANEL, PUT MAIL ON SATCHEL,  PRESS DISPENSER BUTTON [12],
FLIP SWITCH,  (NOTE WHICH WORD AND  WHICH VERSE THE PASSWORD  IS!) WAIT, WAIT,
WAIT, WAIT,  (LISTEN CARFULLY TO THE  PASSWORD) WAIT, WAIT, WAIT,  WAIT, WAIT,
ENJOY POETRY [15], WAIT, WAIT, WAIT,  WAIT, WAIT, TYPE "[PASSWORD WORD]" [25],
GET PLOTTER,GOWN AND TOWEL, WEAR GOWN, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, PRESS GREEN

HEART OF GOLD (FIRST VISIT)
---------------------------
WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, LISTEN, S, PUT ALL IN THING, GET PINCER, PUT PINCER IN
THING, D, W,  TOUCH PAD, GET ATS, E, S,  S, S, S, S, S, LOOK,  LOOK [25], DROP
THING, GET DRIVE,RASP AND PLIERS, N, N,  U, DROP DRIVE, DROP ATS, DROP PLOTTER
(IF YOU DON'T  HAVE THESE THINGS, WAIT A  TURN OR TWO), PUT ALL  IN THING, PUT
SMALL PLUG IN SMALL RECEPTACLE, PUT BIT IN ATS, TURN ON DRIVE

TRAAL — ENTER VIA: SMELL DARK / "BEING WAVED"
---------------------------------------------
WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, SMELL, EXAMINE SHADOW, SAY "arthur", E, GET STONE, PUT
TOWEL ON  HEAD, CARVE "arthur" ON  MEMORIAL, REMOVE TOWEL, DROP  STONE, W, SW,
GET INTERFACE [25], WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT,  WAIT, LISTEN, S, S, U, PUT ALL IN
THING

MISSILE ATTACK (DO THIS AS SOON AS YOU HAVE THE INTERFACE)
----------------------------------------------------------
D, W,  OPEN PANEL, GET  BOARD, INSERT INTERFACE  IN PANEL, CLOSE  PANEL, TOUCH
PAD,  E, U,  PUT LARGE  PLUG IN  LARGE RECEPTACLE,  WAIT, WAIT,  WAIT (MISSILE
ATTACK BEGINS), PANIC, TURN ON DRIVE (MISSILES  BECOME A WHALE), D, W, GET TEA
(NO TEA DROPPED!), E,  U, DROP BOARD AND TEA (NO TEA  PICKED UP!), REMOVE BIT,
PUT BIT IN TEA, TURN ON DRIVE

(TURNING ON THE  DRIVE SENDS YOU TO THE  "DARK". AS WE HAVE THE  REAL TEA, THE
DARK CAN BE CONTROLED.  THERE ARE FIVE SENSES THAT YOU  HAVE: SEE, HEAR, FEEL,
TASTE AND  SMELL. ONE OF THESE  SENSES YOU WILL  HAVE, THE REST YOU  WONT. THE
SENSE WILL APPEAR RANDOMLY. THERE ARE FIVE LOCATIONS THAT YOU MUST VISIT. WAIT
UNTIL YOU GET THE RIGHT SENSE TO JUMP TO THE FOLLOWING FIVE LOCATIONS:

TRILLIAN'S EARTH: FEEL / COLD
THE WHALE: FEEL / WARM
DAMOGRAN: SEE / BACK
FORD'S EARTH: SEE / FRONT
WAR CHAMBER: LISTEN / BELOW
TRALL: SMELL / WAVING

ONCE YOU HAVE  COMPETED THE LOCATION AND  END UP IN THE HEART  OF GOLD BRIDGE,
"TURN ON DRIVE" TO JUMP TO THE NEXT LOCATION. IF YOU END UP IN A LOCATION THAT
YOU HAVE ALREADY BEEN, YOU WILL BE SENT BACK TO THE DARK IN THE HEART OF GOLD.
GO S, S, U, TO RETURN TO THE BRIDGE.

TRILLIAN'S EARTH — ENTER VIA: TOUCH DARK / "COLD"
-------------------------------------------------
FEEL (IT FEELS COLD), DRINK LIQUID, EXAMINE ARTHUR, DROP WINE, GET FLUFF, OPEN
BAG, PUT FLUFF IN  BAG, GET WINE, WAIT (UNTIL PHIL  ARRIVES) [25], WAIT, WAIT,
WAIT, WAIT, LISTEN, S, S, U, OPEN BAG, GET ALL FROM BAG, PUT ALL IN THING

THE WHALE — ENTER VIA: TOUCH DARK / "WARM" (REQUIRES REAL TEA)
--------------------------------------------------------------
FEEL (IT  FEELS WARM), DRINK  LIQUID, DROP THING, GET  POT, PUT POT  IN THING,
WAIT (UNTIL YOU GO SPLATT!!!!!), WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, LISTEN, S, S, U,

DAMOGRAN (AS ZAPHOD) — ENTER VIA: SEE DARK / "BACK"
---------------------------------------------------
SEE (PAINFUL LIGHT AT THE BACK OF  YOUR EYES), EXAMINE LIGHT, LOOK UNDER SEAT,
UNLOCK BOX WITH KEY, GET ALL FROM BOX, PUSH BUTTON, STEER TOWARDS SPIRE, WAIT,
WAIT, WAIT,  STAND, EXIT, WAVE,  WAIT (UNTIL TRILLIAN ARRIVES),  "GUARDS, DROP
RIFLES", "TRILLIAN, SHOOT RIFLES", E, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, LISTEN, S, S, S,
D, GET FLUFF, DROP THING, GET WRENCH AND GLASS, PUT ALL IN THING, U, N, U

FORD'S EARTH — ENTER VIA: SEE DARK / "FRONT"
--------------------------------------------
SEE (PAINFUL LIGHT AT THE FRONT OF YOUR EYES), EXAMINE LIGHT, N, OPEN SATCHEL,
GET FLUFF  AND TOWEL,  OFFER TOWEL  TO ARTHUR,  IDIOT, WALK  AROUND BULLDOZER,
"PROSSER, LIE  IN MUD", S,  W, BUY BEER, BUY  PEANUTS, DRINK BEER,  DRINK BEER
[15], E, N, GIVE FLUFF TO ARTHUR, WAIT (UNTIL BACK IN DARK), WAIT, WAIT, WAIT,
WAIT, LISTEN, S, S, U

WAR CHAMBER / MAZE — ENTER VIA: HEAR DARK / "BELOW"
---------------------------------------------------
HEAR DARK, S, GET AWL, PUT ALL IN  THING, WAIT (UNTIL YOU ARRIVE AT THE MAZE),
N (KEEP GOING NORTH UNTIL YOU FIND A BLACK PARTICLE), GET PARTICLE [25], WAIT,
WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, LISTEN, S, S, U

HEART OF GOLD (FINAL SECTION)
-----------------------------
(MAKE SURE  ALL FIVE SCENARIOS HAVE  BEEN COMPLETED BEFORE THIS  SECTION), PUT
ALL IN THING, REMOVE BIT, GET TEA AND NO TEA, (YOU MIGHT NEED TO GET THE FLUFF
FIRST), PUT SEAT FLUFF  IN POT, PUT JACKET FLUFF IN POT,  PUT SATCHEL FLUFF IN
POT, PUT  POCKET FLUFF IN  POT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT,  W [25], EXAMINE  PLANT, GET
FRUIT, D, S, OPEN  DOOR, DRINK TEA [100], W [25], DROP  THING, GET CHISEL, PUT
CHISEL IN THING,  "MARVIN, OPEN HATCH", E,  D, EAT FRUIT (DROP  ALL EXCEPT FOR
TOOL THAT  YOU SAW  GAVE TO MARVIN),  DROP ALL, GET  (CORRECT TOOL),  E, WAIT,
WAIT, GIVE (CORRECT TOOL) TO MARVIN, W, D

(YOU WIN! WITH 400/400 POINTS)
