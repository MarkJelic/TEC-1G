AIM: TO FIND 10 TREASURES AND BE IN THE DINING ROOM BY 9AM THE NEXT
MORNING

OUTSIDE
-------
TURN STATUE W, TURN  STATUE E, TURN  STATUE N, N, OPEN BOX, GET  YELLOW,
OPEN DOOR, TURN TORCH ON, IN

FIX STAIRS
----------
OPEN CLOSET, IN, PULL  THIRD PEG, OPEN  DOOR, OUT, TURN  NEWEL, IN, PULL
SECOND PEG

FIRE HYDRANT
------------
GET BUCKET, OPEN  DOOR, OUT, N, UNLOCK  DOOR, OPEN DOOR, N, GET CARD, N,
NW, NE, FILL BUCKET, SE, SW, S, S, S, IN, HANG BUCKET  ON THIRD, OUT, U,
OPEN DOOR, IN, WAIT, WAIT, OPEN DOOR, N, OPEN PANEL, OPEN TRUNK, GET
HYDRANT [10], DROP HYDRANT, D 

FINCH
-----
W, S, LOOK  UNDER MAT, GET CARD, N, E, E, HOLD  SACK, OPEN  WINDOW, OPEN
SACK [10], GET FINCH, DROP SACK AND FINCH

PENGUIN
-------
W, D, W, GET RED  CANDLE, IN, GET BRICK, DROP  BRICK, GET CARD, U, U, U,
E, D, GET PENGUIN [10], DROP PENGUIN, U, W, D, D, D, OUT

DIAMOND RING
------------
N, E, PRESS GREEN, AGAIN, AGAIN, PRESS BLACK, AGAIN, PRESS WHITE, AGAIN,
PRESS GREEN, AGAIN, AGAIN, PRESS BLACK, PRESS BLUE, PRESS  GREEN, AGAIN,
AGAIN, AGAIN, PRESS RED, AGAIN, AGAIN, GET RING [10], DROP RING

CHEESE GRATER
-------------
E, E, GET CARD, S, GET  STRIP AND  SLIDE, PUT STRIP  IN FILM  PROJECTOR,
PUT  SLIDE  IN  SLIDE  PROJECTOR, REMOVE  CAP, DROP  CAP, TURN  ON SLIDE
PROJECTOR, FOCUS  IT, TURN ON FILM  PROJECTOR, LOOK AT SCREEN [10] (NOTE
THE SONG NAME), N, W, W, S, E, MOVE  PAINTING, GET CARD, TURN DIAL RIGHT
3, TURN  DIAL LEFT 7, TURN  DIAL  RIGHT 5, OPEN  SAFE, GET  GRATER [10],
DROP GRATER

PARKING METER
-------------
E, OPEN PIANO, GET CARD, PLAY "(SONG NAME  ABOVE)", PUSH  PIANO N, D, S,
GET  PILLAR, DROP  PILLAR, N,  U, PUSH  PIANO S, AGAIN, D, N, GET  METER
[10], DROP METER

TOUPEE
------
S, U, W, W, W, N, GET  PAPER, W, GET  MATCHBOX, D, OPEN  CLOSET, S,  GET
CARD, N, TURN  COMPUTER  ON, PUT RED  CARD IN  SLOT, PUT ORANGE  CARD IN
SLOT, PUT YELLOW CARD IN SLOT, PUT GREEN CARD  IN SLOT, PUT BLUE CARD IN
SLOT, PUT  INDIGO  CARD IN  SLOT, PUT VIOLET CARD  IN SLOT, U, E, S, GET
CALL 576-3190, N, W, D, GET TOUPEE [10], DROP TOUPEE

STAMP
-----
U, E, S, E, U, OPEN  CLOSET, S, GET SKIS, N, D, N, N, N, NW, GET SHOVEL,
NE, N, PUT THIN ON YELLOW, DROP THIN AND YELLOW, N, N, W, N, W, W, N, W,
S, W, W, N, W, S, E, S, E, N, E, S, W, N, W, S, W, N, W, S, W, N, E,  N,
E, N, E, E, N, E, S, E, E, S, E, N, E, N, E, S, W, S, W, S, E, N, W,  S,
DIG WITH  SHOVEL, GET STAMP [10], DROP STAMP AND  SHOVEL, N, E, S, W, N,
E, N, E, N, W, S, W, S, W, N, W, W, N, W, S, W, W, S, W, S, W, S, E,  N,
E, S, E, N, E, S, E, N, W, S, W, N, W, N, E, S, E, E, N, E, S, E, E,  S,
E, S, S

MASK
----
W, N, GET BALL, PUT  BALL IN  CANNON, OPEM  MATCHBOX, GET  MATCH,  LIGHT
MATCH, LIGHT FUSE, DROP MATCHBOX, OPEN  COMPARTMENT, GET MASK [10], DROP
MASK

FILM REEL
---------
N, TURN LIGHT OFF, DROP LIGHT, S, E, E, WEAR SKIS, D [10], REMOVE  SKIS,
DROP SKIS, GET MATCH, LIGHT CANDLE  WITH FIRE, DRIP WAX  ONTO MATCH, PUT
OUT CANDLE, S, W, SWIM, D, D, W, U, U, N, LIGHT  MATCH, LIGHT CANDLE, N,
U, PULL CHAIN, BURN ROPE, LOWER RIGHT END  OF PLANK, STAND ON  RIGHT END 
OF PLANK, PUT OUT CANDLE, DROP CANDLE, GET LADDER, GET LIGHT, TURN LIGHT
ON,  D, GET  LADDER, PUT  LADDER ON  HOOKS, TURN DIAL  LEFT 4, TURN DIAL
RIGHT 5, TURN DIAL LEFT 7, OPEN SAFE, GET REEL [10], DROP REEL, GET PEG, 

FINAL CHALLENGE
---------------
U, U, W, E, S, SE, SW, S, S, S, U, IN, PUT PEG IN HOLE [10], GET  SWORD,
KILL HERMAN  WITH SWORD, AGAIN, GET CLUB (OR ANOTHER  FREE WEAPON), KILL
HERMAN WITH CLUB, GET STICK (OR ANOTHER  FREE WEAPON), KILL  HERMAN WITH
STICK, TURN SAW OFF [20]

(YOU WIN! WITH 150/150 POINTS)
