AIM: TO FIND A PYRAMID AND TAKE ITS TREASURES WITHIN

DESERT
------
STAND, S, S, GET MATCHBOOK, S, S, GET  SACK, N, NW, W, GET ALL, E, N, W,
DROP SACK, GET CANTEEN, OPEN CANTEEN, FILL  CANTEEN, CLOSE  CANTEEN, PUT
ALL IN SACK, GET  SACK, E, E, N, BREAK LOCK  WITH SHOVEL  [5], GET LOCK,
DROP LOCK, OPEN TRUNK, GET  BEEF AND  MAP, UNFOLD  MAP, GET CUBE, S, SE,
SE, E, DIG SAND WITH  SHOVEL, AGAIN, AGAIN,  AGAIN, AGAIN [25], PUT CUBE
IN OPENING [20], DROP ALL EXCEPT BEEF AND SACK, D

CHAMBER OF RA
-------------
GET ALL, OPEN JAR, POUR LIQUID ON  TORCH, CLOSE JAR, DROP  SACK, PUT JAR
IN SACK, GET CANTEEN,MATCHBOOK AND  ROPE, EAT BEEF, OPEN CANTEEN, DRINK,
CLOSE  CANTEEN,  LIGHT  MATCH,  LIGHT  TORCH,  DROP  CANTEEN  MATCH  AND
MATCHBOOK, GET SACK, TIE ROPE TO ALTER, THROW ROPE NORTH, N [30]

CIRCULAR ROOM

PUSH STATUE, GET HEAD, PUSH  STATUE NW, DROP  HEAD, SE, SE, SE [25], GET
CLUSTER [5], NW, NW, NW, GET HEAD, PUSH STATUE SE, AGAIN, DROP HEAD, NW,
NW, NW [25], GET CLUSTER [5], SE, SE, SE, GET HEAD, PUSH STATUE NW, PUSH
STATUE NE, DROP HEAD, SW, SW, SW [25], GET  CLUSTER [5], NE, NE, NE, GET
HEAD, PUSH  STATUE SW, AGAIN, DROP  HEAD, NE,  NE, NE [25], GET  CLUSTER
[5], SW, SW, DROP SACK, PUT ALL CLUSTERS IN SACK, GET SACK, U

THE BARGE CHAMBER

S, S, NE, NW, N, E, D, W, PUT  TORCH IN  KNOTHOLE, GET  SHIM, DROP SHIM,
LIFT THE BEAM, GET  TORCH, E, U, W, GET  BEAM [15], S, W, N, E, N, N, N, 
N, N, E, S, GET CHALICE [15], N, W, W, S, GET  CHALICE [15], N, E, S, S,
S, S, S, E, S, W, SE, SW, N, N

THE CUBE LEVEL

GET CANTEEN, E, W, S, TAKE FIRST, THIRD AND FIFTH  BRICK [25], DROP  ALL 
BRICKS, E, N, W, DROP  SACK, GET  AXE, GET  SACK, HIT  PLASTER  WITH AXE
[10], W, W, W, PUT BEAM  IN NICHE, STAND ON  BEAM, HIT PLASTER  WITH AXE
[25], OPEN DOOR, W

ANTECHAMGER

GET BEAM, S, PUT BEAM IN DOORWAY, OPEN  DOOR, W, DROP  SACK, PUT DIAMOND
IN FIRST, PUT RUBY  IN SECOND, PUT EMERALD IN THIRD, PUT OPAL IN FOURTH,
LIFT SLAB, GET BOOK [10], GET JAR, OPEN JAR, POUR  LIQUID ON TORCH, DROP
JAR, E, GET BEAM, N, N, PUT BEAM UNDER LINTEL, BREAK SEAL WITH AXE, DROP 
AXE, OPEN  DOOR [40], N, E, OPEN  CANTEEN, FILL SILVER  WITH  WATER, PUT
SILVER ON LEFT DISC, PUT CHALICE ON  RIGHT DISC, GET SCARAB [10], W, PUT
SCARAB  IN SMALL  RECESS, PUT  BOOK  IN LARGE  RECESS, TURN  NEITH, TURN
SELKIS, TURN ISIS, TURN NEPHTHYS [35], OPEN SARCOPHAGUS

(YOU WIN (OR LOSE)! WITH 400/400 POINTS)
