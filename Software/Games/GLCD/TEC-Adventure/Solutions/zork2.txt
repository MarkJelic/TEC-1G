AIM: TO DEFEAT THE WIZARD OF FROBOZZ

NOTE: THE  WIZARD APPEARS RANDOMLY AND  DOES SOME ANNOYING THINGS  TO YOUR. IF
WHAT HE DOES STUFFS YOU AROUND, SAVE OFTEN AND THEN RESTORE.

GET TO CAROUSEL ROOM
--------------------
GET ALL, S, S, S, SW, S, SE, IN, GET ALL, OUT, S, S, W, LIGHT LAMP,

(THE CAROUSEL  ROOM CONTINUALLY SPINS,  EXITING IS RANDOM,  IF YOU EXIT  TO AN
INCORRECT ROOM, GO BACK TO THE CAROUSEL AND TRY AGAIN. USE THESE DIRECTIONS TO
GO BACK.  MARBLE HALL:S, COOL  ROOM:SE, ROOM 8:E, COBWEBBY:NE,  MENHIR ROOM:N,
RIDDLE ROOM:NW, TOPLARY:W, PATH:SW)

GET SPHERE AND KILL DRAGON
--------------------------
N (KEEP TRYING TIL ARRIVE AT MARBLE HALL),  GET BRICK, N, GET WATER, N, U, PUT
MAT UNDER DOOR, MOVE LID, PUT OPENER IN KEYHOLE, PULL MAT, GET KEY, GET OPENER
AND DROP IT, UNLOCK DOOR WITH KEY, OPEN DOOR, DROP KEY, N, GET SPHERE [20], S,
D, W, N, HIT  DRAGON WITH SWORD, S, HIT DRAGON WITH SWORD,  S, HIT DRAGON WITH
SWORD, W [5], U, S, GET STRING, DROP SWORD AND SPHERE

FLY A BALLOON AND GET TREASURES ALONG THE WAY
---------------------------------------------
N, D, W,  S, OPEN RECEPTACLE, PUT NEWSPAPER IN  RECEPTACLE, LIGHT MATCH, LIGHT
NEWSPAPER WITH MATCH,  ENTER BASKET, WAIT, WAIT, LAND, TIE  ROPE TO HOOK, EXIT
BASKET, S, GET PURPLE BOOK, N, GET  COIN [20], ENTER BALLOON, UNTIE ROPE, OPEN
BOOK, GET  STAMP [10],  DROP BOOK, WAIT,  WAIT, LAND, TIE  ROPE TO  HOOK, EXIT
BASKET, S, PUT BRICK  IN HOLE, PUT STRING IN BRICK,  LIGHT MATCH, LIGHT STRING
WITH  MATCH,  N,  S, GET  CROWN  [20],  N,  ENTER  BASKET, UNTIE  ROPE,  CLOSE
RECEPTACLE, DROP  MATCHES, WAIT, WAIT,  WAIT, WAIT,  EXIT BASKET, N,  GET RUBY
[15], E, U, S, DROP CROWN,RUBY,STAMP AND COIN

GET PORTRAIT AND BILLS
----------------------
N, D,  E, N, N, W,  W, NE, E, S,  GET PORTRAIT [15], N,  WALK THROUGH CURTAIN,
WALK THROUGH  SOUTH WALL, WALK THROUGH  CURTAIN, GET BILLS [25],  WALK THROUGH
NORTH WALL,  DROP PORTRAIT AND  BILLS, E, E, GET  ALL BUT PAPER,  WALK THROUGH
CURTAIN, S, E, E, N

FOLLOW PRINCESS
---------------
OPEN CHEST,  OPEN CHEST, (FOLLOW  PRINCESS TO GAZEBO,  YOU MIGHT NEED  TO WAIT
UNTIL SHE MOVES), S, E,  E, D, S, E, E, N, IN, WAIT, WAIT  [15], OUT, S, W, W,
N, N, W, N, N,  DROP ROSE, GET STATUE [20], S, S, S, W,  U, S, DROP ALL EXCEPT
LAMP AND POT

RIDE BUCKET AND GET ROBOT TO HELP
---------------------------------
NE, SE  (KEEP TRYING TIL ARRIVE  AT RIDDLE ROOM),  ANSWER "A WELL" [5],  E, E,
ENTER BUCKET, DROP WATER [10], EXIT BUCKET,  E, GET ALL BUT ORANGE, EAT GREEN,
E, DROP  RED IN POOL,  GET PACKAGE [15],  W, EAT BLUE,  NW, "ROBOT, GO  E", E,
"ROBOT, PRESS TRIANGULAR  BUTTON", "ROBOT, GO S", S, GET  SPHERE, "ROBOT, LIFT
CAGE", GET SPHERE  [20], N, W, SE (KEEP  TRYING TO GET TO TEA  ROOM), W, ENTER
BUCKET, GET WATER,  EXIT BUCKET, W, DROP  POT, GET NECKLACE [15],  W, NW, OPEN
BOX, GET VIOLIN [20]

AWAKEN A DEMON AND GIVE HIM ALL YOUR TREASURES
----------------------------------------------
SW, GET  KEY,SWORD AND SPHERE,  SW, GIVE PACKAGE  TO LIZARD, UNLOCK  DOOR WITH
KEY, OPEN DOOR, S [10], W, W, THROW SWORD AT AQUARIUM, GET SPHERE [20], E, PUT
CLEAR IN  DIAMOND, PUT  RED IN RUBY  STAND, PUT BLUE  IN SAPPHIRE,  GET SPHERE
[30], S, PUT BLACK  IN PENTAGRAM, GIVE ALL BUT LAMP TO DEMON  [6], N, E, N, N,
GET ALL,  SW, S, W,  S, GIVE ALL  BUT LAMP TO  DEMON [14], "DEMON,GIVE  ME THE
WAND", GET WAND [30], N, E, N, N, NE

FINAL ESCAPE
------------
W, GET CAN, E,  S, POINT WAND AT MENHIR AND SAY "FLOAT",  SW, GET COLLAR [15],
NE, S, D, W (SHOULD SEE CLUB, IF NOT GO E AND W), SE, NE, NW, SW, (NOW GO IN A
DIRECTION THAT YOU  CAN N/S/E/W), (SECRET PASSAGE DOWN) D  [10], PUT COLLAR ON
DOG, E [3], OPEN DOOR, S [2], SPRAY  ME WITH CAN, DOUSE LAMP, OPEN SOUTH DOOR,
S

(YOU WIN! WITH 400/400 POINTS)
