AIM: TO COLLECT 19 TREASURES AND PLACE THEM IN A DISPLAY CASE

NOTES: THE THIEF  MIGHT TAKE SOME OF YOUR TREASURES  AND/OR FIGHT YOU EARLIER.
YOU WILL GET THE TREASURES BACK WHEN HE DIES

GET EGG AND GET READY TO EXPLORE
--------------------------------

N, N, U, GET EGG  [5], D, S, E, OPEN WINDOW, IN [10], W,  GET ALL, E, U, LIGHT
LAMP, GET ALL, D, W, MOVE RUG, OPEN TRAPDOOR, D [25]

KILL TROLL, THIEF AND OPEN EGG
------------------------------

N,  KILL TROLL  WITH SWORD  (REPEAT  UNTIL DEAD),  DROP  SWORD, S,  S, E,  GET
PAINTING [4], W, N, N, W, W, W, U, GET BAG [10], SW, E, S, SE, ODYSSEUS, (SAVE
GAME HERE  IF THIEF KILLS  YOU), U  [25], GIVE EGG  TO THIEF, KILL  THIEF WITH
KNIFE (REPEAT UNTIL DEAD OR IF YOU DIE, RESTORE GAME), DROP KNIFE, GET CHALICE
AND EGG [10], D, E, E, OPEN CASE, PUT PAINTING,CHALICE AND BAG IN CASE [16]

GET BAUBLE
----------

TURN LAMP OFF, E, E, E, N, W, WIND UP CANARY, GET BAUBLE AND CANARY [7], S, E,
IN, W, PUT EGG,BAUBLE AND CANARY IN CASE [10]

GET COFFIN
----------

OPEN TRAPDOOR, D, LIGHT LAMP, N, E [5],  E, SE, E, TIE ROPE TO RAILING, D, GET
TORCH [14],  TURN LAMP OFF,  S, DROP LANTERN, E,  OPEN COFFIN, GET  COFFIN AND
SCEPTRE [14], W, TEMPLE, D, E, E, PUT COFFIN IN CASE [15]

GET POT OF GOLD
---------------

E, E, E, E, D, D, N, WAVE SCEPTRE, LOOK,  GET POT [10], SW, U, U, NW, W, W, W,
PUT POT AND SCEPTRE IN CASE [16]

GET TREASURE CHEST AND PLATINUM BAR
-----------------------------------

D, N,  E, E, E,  ECHO, GET BAR  [10], U,  E, N, GET  MATCH, N, GET  WRENCH AND
SCREWDRIVER, PRESS YELLOW, S, S, TURN  BOLT WITH WRENCH, DROP WRENCH, W, WAIT,
WAIT, N, GET TRUNK [15], N, GET PUMP, S,  S, SE, D, W, SE, E, D, S, TEMPLE, D,
E, E, PUT BAR AND TRUNK IN CASE [10]

SCARE OFF SOME GHOSTS AND GO FOR A BOAT RIDE
--------------------------------------------

W, W,  U, TEMPLE, GET  ALL, S, GET  ALL, D, D,  RING BELL, LIGHT  MATCH, LIGHT
CANDLES, READ PRAYER, S, DROP BOOK AND CANDLES, GET SKULL [10], N, U, N, N, N,
E, U, E, D, INFLATE BOAT WITH PUMP, DROP PUMP, ENTER BOAT, LAUNCH, WAIT, WAIT,
WAIT, WAIT, GET BUOY, E, GET OUT OF  BOAT, DROP BUOY, OPEN IT [5], GET EMERALD
AND SHOVEL, NE, DIG IN SAND WITH SHOVEL, AGAIN, AGAIN, AGAIN, DROP SHOVEL, GET
SCARAB [5], SW, S, S, W, W, SW, U,  U, NW, W, W, OPEN SACK, GET GARLIC, W, PUT
SKULL,EMERALD AND SCARAB IN CASE [25]

MAKE A DIAMOND AND COLLECT THE REST OF THE TREASURES
----------------------------------------------------

D, N, E, N,  NE, N, N, N, GET TRIDENT  [4], U, N, N, W, N, W,  N, E, PUT TORCH
AND SCREWDRIVER IN BASKET, N, LIGHT LAMP,  D, GET BRACELET [5], E, NE, SE, SW,
D, D,  S, GET  COAL, N, U,  U, N,  E, S, N,  U, S, PUT  COAL IN  BASKET, LOWER
BASKET, N, D, E, NE,  SE, SW, D, D, W, DROP ALL, W,  GET ALL FROM BASKET [13],
S, OPEN  LID, PUT COAL  IN MACHINE, CLOSE  LID, TURN SWITCH  WITH SCREWDRIVER,
OPEN LID, GET DIAMOND [10], DROP SCREWDRIVER, N, PUT ALL IN BASKET, E, GET ALL
BUT TIMBER, E, U, U,  N, E, S, N, U, S, RAISE BASKET,  GET ALL FROM BASKET, W,
GET FIGURINE  [5], S,  E, S, D,  U, PUT  FIGURINE,DIAMOND,TRIDENT,BRACELET AND
TORCH IN CASE [37], E, E, S, W, SW, ENTER BARROW

(YOU WIN! WITH 350/350 POINTS)
