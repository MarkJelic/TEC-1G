AIM: TO  SURVIVE ON  AN ABANDON  RESEARCH BASE AND  REPAIR KEY  SYSTEMS TO
ESCAPE

ON THE FEINSTEIN
----------------

WAIT, WAIT, WAIT,  WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT,  W, GET IN WEBBING,
WAIT [3]  (UNTIL THE POD  LANDS), GET OUT, DROP  ALL, GET KIT,  OPEN DOOR,
EXIT, SWIM UP [3], U, U, U

GET PADLOCK KEY
---------------

N, NE, E, E,  E, S, S, S, SW, DROP  ID, GET BAR, NE, N, N,  N, N, HOLD BAR
OVER CREVICE, DROP BAR, S, OPEN KIT, DROP KIT

FIND LADDER AND CARDS
---------------------

W, W, UNLOCK PADLOCK, GET PADLOCK, DROP ALL, OPEN DOOR, N [4], GET LADDER,
S, E, E, N,  N, DROP LADDER, OPEN LADDER, PUT LADDER OVER  RIFT, N [4], W,
W, OPEN DESK, GET CARD [1], E, OPEN DESK, GET KITCHEN AND UPPER CARDS [2]

FIX COMMUNICATION SYSTEMS
-------------------------

E, S,  S, S, GET KIT,  EAT BROWN, DROP KIT,  E, PUSH BLUE, WAIT,  WAIT, N,
SLIDE UPPER THROUGH SLOT, PUSH UP BUTTON, WAIT, WAIT, S [4], NE

GET LIQUID THAT MATCHES COLOUR LIGHT (REPEAT IF NECESSARY)
----------------------------------------------------------

(REMEMBER FLASHING  LIGHT COLOUR ON  ENUNCIATOR PANEL. THIS IS  THE LIQUID
YOU NEED), SW, N, SLIDE UPPER  THROUGH SLOT, PUSH DOWN BUTTON, WAIT, WAIT,
S, W, S, S, S, (ON FIRST GO:  SW, GET FLASK, E), (ON REPEAT: S), PUT FLASK
UNDER SPOUT,  PUSH (COLOUR),  GET FLASK,  N, N,  N, N,  E, N,  SLIDE UPPER
THROUGH SLOT,  PUSH UP  BUTTON, WAIT,  WAIT, S, NE,  POUR LIQUID  IN HOLE,
(REPEAT FLASK FILLING UNTIL NO MORE COLOUR LIGHTS APPEAR) [6]

SLEEP
-----

SW, N, SLIDE UPPER THROUGH SLOT, PUSH DOWN BUTTON, DROP UPPER, DROP FLASK,
(WAIT IF DOOR HASN'T  OPENED), S, W, GET KIT, EAT RED  (IF NOT HUNGRY JUST
WAIT UNTIL YOU ARE), DROP KIT, W, N, GET IN BED, SLEEP, SLEEP

DRINK AND GET SOME ELECTRONICS AND GET FLOYD
--------------------------------------------

GET UP, GET ALL, S, W, S, GET CANTEEN, OPEN CANTEEN, SLIDE KITCHEN THROUGH
SLOT, DROP KITCHEN, S [4], PUT CANTEEN IN NICHE, PUSH BUTTON, GET CANTEEN,
N, N, E, E, S,  E, GET BEDISTOR, W, S, S, SW, GET  LASER AND PLIERS, E, E,
SEARCH ROBOT [1],  TURN ON ROBOT [2],  NW, N, N, N, DROP  PLIERS, GET KIT,
EAT GREEN, DROP KIT, GET PLIERS, CLOSE CANTEEN, E


TAKE THE SHUTTLE
----------------

PUSH RED, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, S, SLIDE LOWER THROUGH SLOT, PUSH DOWN BUTTON,
DROP LOWER, WAIT, WAIT,  WAIT, N, E [4], S, E,  SLIDE SHUTTLE CARD THROUGH
SLOT, DROP SHUTTLE  CARD, PUSH LEVER UP, WAIT (REPEAT  UNTIL SPEED REACHES
55), PULL  LEVER DOWN, WAIT,  WAIT, PULL  LEVER DOWN, WAIT  (UNTIL SHUTTLE
COMES TO A REST)

FIX PLANETARY DEFENSE
---------------------

W, N [4], U, U, NE, N, TELL FLOYD  TO GO N, TELL FLOYD TO GET SHINY BOARD,
S, E, N, OPEN PANEL, REMOVE SECOND BOARD, INSERT SHINY BOARD IN PANEL [6],
DROP FRIED BOARD

FIX COURSE CONTROL
------------------

S, E, N, OPEN LID, REMOVE FUSED BEDISTOR WITH PLIERS, INSERT GOOD BEDISTOR
IN CUBE [6], DROP FUSED BEDISTOR AND PLIERS

FIX LASER AND GET MINIATURIZATION CARD
--------------------------------------

S, S, S,  S, , (WAIT UNTIL  FLOYD ENTERS AND NOTICES  COMPUTER IS BUSTED),
NE, OPEN  BIO-LOCK, SE, E,  LOOK IN WINDOW,  OPEN DOOR, CLOSE  DOOR, LOOK,
OPEN DOOR, CLOSE  DOOR [2], GET CARD  [1], W, OPEN DOOR, W,  S, REMOVE OLD
BATTERY, DROP BATTERY, INSERT NEW BATTERY IN LASER, N, SW, S


KILL MONSTER
------------

DRINK LIQUID, DROP CANTEEN, SLIDE  MINIATURIZATION THROUGH SLOT, TYPE 384,
SET DIAL TO  1, E [4], N,  N, SHOOT BOULDER WITH LASER  [2], (REPEAT UNTIL
BOULDER IS  DESTROYED) [8], SET  DIAL TO 6,  S, SHOOT MONSTER  WITH LASER,
(REPEAT UNTIL  MONSTER IS ATTRACTED  TO HEAT  OF LASER), THROW  LASER INTO
VOID

FINAL ESCAPE
------------

S, W [4], N, OPEN DESK, WEAR MASK,  PUSH RED, OPEN DOOR, W, OPEN LAB DOOR,
W, W, OPEN DOOR, W, SW, W, S, PUSH BUTTON [5], WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, N

(YOU WIN! WITH 80/80 POINTS)
