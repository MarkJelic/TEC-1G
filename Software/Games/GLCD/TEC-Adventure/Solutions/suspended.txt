AIM: TO SAVE THE INHABITANTS ON THE PLANET SURFACE.

SET UP ROBOTS
-------------
POET,  GO TO  WEATHER CONTROL.  SENSA,  GO TO  SUB SUPPLY.  WHIZ, GO  TO
SECONDARY CHANNEL. SENSA, GET RAMP.  WEST. GET CONTAINER AND GRASPER. GO
TO HALLWAY  JUNCTION. WALDO, GO TO  HALLWAY JUNCTION. AUDA, GO  TO GAMMA
REPAIR.

FIX WEATHER
-----------
POET, TURN SECOND DIAL  TO 100 [1]. GO TO HALLWAY END.  IRIS, GO TO MAIN
SUPPLY. SENSA, PUT RAMP AT DROPOFF. WALDO, GET CONTAINER AND GRASPER. GO
TO MAIN  SUPPLY [-1].  INSTALL GRASPER.  GET RED  AND YELLOW  IC. SENSA,
NORTH. GET RAMP.

FIX IRIS AND GET CAMERA
-----------------------
GO TO SMALL  SUPPLY. WALDO, OPEN PANEL. REPLACE ROUGH  DEVICE WITH ROUGH
OBJECT. CLOSE  PANEL. POET, GET  IN CAR. GET OUT  OF CAR. GO  TO BIOLOGY
LAB. WALDO, GET BURNED AND FRIED CHIP. POET, GET CAMERA. SENSA, PUT RAMP
AT HOLDER.

GET TOOL AND FIX MACHINE
------------------------
GET ON RAMP. GET CUTTER. GET OFF RAMP. GET RAMP. GO TO SLOPING CORRIDOR.
POET, GO  TO VEHICLE DEBARKATION. WALDO,  PUT RED IC IN  RED SOCKET. PUT
YELLOW IC IN YELLOW SOCKET. POET, GET IN CAR. GET OUT OF CAR.

GET FIRST CABLE
---------------
GO TO PRIMARY  CHANNEL. SENSA, PUT RAMP AT DROPOFF.  GO TO GAMMA REPAIR.
WALDO, PUSH  BUTTON. IRIS, GET FUSE.  WALDO, GET CABLE. GO  TO SECONDARY
CHANNEL. WAIT.

GET ACCESS CODES
----------------
SENSA, EXAMINE OBJECT. TURN FLOWSWITCH.  BOTH SENSA AND AUDA, MOVE FRED.
CUT CABLE WITH CUTTER. POET, PLUG  IN CAMERA. POINT CAMERA AT SIGN (NOTE
WHAT IS SAYS: IE MUMBLE)

GET SECOND CABLE
----------------
SENSA, GET CABLE.  GO TO PRIMARY CHANNEL. WAIT.  WHIZ, GET FOURTEEN-INCH
CABLE.  REPLACE NINE-INCH  CABLE WITH  FOURTEEN-INCH CABLE.  WAIT. WAIT.
SENSA, REPLACE FOUR-INCH CABLE WITH TWELVE-INCH CABLE.

RESET SURFACE SYSTEM
--------------------
IRIS, PUT  FUSE  IN  MACHINE. PRESS (FIRST HALF OF  ACCESS CODE) CIRCLE, PRESS (SECOND HALF OF ACCESS CODE) CIRCLE.

(ACCESS CODES ARE MAPPED TO THE SIGN WORD. PRESS THE BUTTONS IN ORDER OF
THE LETTER COMBINATIONS: FOO, BAR, BOZ, MUM, BLE, KLA, CON, TRA)

(YOU WIN! WITH 0 (DEATHS) IN 70 MOVES)
