AIM: BE  KIND TO OTHERS,  SOLVE THE DUNGEON  MYSTERIES AND BECOME  THE DUNGEON
MASTER!

GET LIGHT SOURCES AND GRUE REPELLENT
------------------------------------
GET LAMP, LIGHT LAMP, S, S, S, S,  DOUSE LAMP, DROP LAMP, JUMP IN LAKE [1], W,
S, GET TORCH, WAIT (UNTIL INDICATOR SHOWS II), TOUCH TABLE [1], GET CAN, E

BE NICE AND GIVE TREASURE CHEST TO MAN
--------------------------------------
WAIT (UNTIL INDICATOR SHOWS III), TOUCH TABLE, DROP TORCH, E, N, JUMP IN LAKE,
D, GET CAN AND SHINY OBJECT (IF YOU DROP ONE OR BOTH, TRY AGAIN), S, SPRAY CAN
ON ME,  S, S, E,  GET KEY, OPEN  COVER, D, N,  N, N, GET  TORCH, W, W,  W, GET
BREAD, CLIMB ROPE [1],  WAIT, WAIT, TIE CHEST TO ROPE,  WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, GRAB
ROPE

BE NICE AND SHOW MERCY ON HOODED FIGURE
---------------------------------------
SW, (WAIT FOR HOODED  FIGURE APPEARS [1], SAVE HERE TOO),  KILL MAN [1], AGAIN
(UNTIL HE IS BADLY HURT AND DEFENCELESS), GET HOOD, GET CLOAK

GO BACK IN TIME TO GET GOLDEN RING
----------------------------------
E, N, E, S, E, E, S, S, (IF  CAN'T GO EAST, WAIT UNTIL EARTHQUAKE), E, N, PUSH
GOLD MACHINE S, OPEN STONE DOOR, PUSH GOLD MACHINE E, SIT ON SEAT, SET DIAL TO
776, PRESS BUTTON [1], GET RING, WAIT (UNTIL GUARDS MARCH AWAY), OPEN DOOR, W,
OPEN WOODEN DOOR, N, LOOK UNDER SEAT, HIDE  RING UNDER  SEAT, WAIT (UNTIL  YOU
RETURN), GET ALL, OPEN STONE DOOR, W, OPEN WOODEN DOOR, N, LOOK UNDER SEAT, S,
S, D

SOLVE A SOKOBAN PUZZLE AND GET ANCIENT BOOK
-------------------------------------------
PUSH E WALL [1], S, S, SE, PUSH S WALL,  N, NE, PUSH S WALL, GET BOOK,  PUSH S
WALL, E, NE, PUSH W WALL, SW, NW, NE, PUSH S WALL, SW, PUSH E WALL, NE, PUSH S
WALL, NW, N, N, N, PUSH E WALL, SW, S,SE, NE, N, PUSH W WALL, NW, PUSH S WALL,
PUSH S WALL, W, NW, NW, PUSH S WALL, SE, SE, SE, NE, PUSH W WALL, PUSH W WALL,
SW, PUSH N WALL, PUSH N WALL, PUSH N WALL, NW, U

BE NICE TO OLD MAN AND SOLVE THE MIRROR PUZZLE
----------------------------------------------
N, W,  N, N, W,  W, N, E,  NE, (IF MAN ISN'T  HERE, REPEAT SW,NE),  WAKE, GIVE
BREAD, OPEN DOOR, N, N, PUT CAN IN  BEAM, S, PUSH BUTTON, N, N, N, RAISE SHORT
POLE, PRESS RED, PRESS RED, LOWER  SHORT POLE, PRESS MAHOGANY, PRESS MAHOGANY,
PRESS MAHOGANY, RAISE SHORT POLE, PRESS  RED, PRESS RED, PRESS RED, PRESS RED,
PRESS PINE, N

BECOME DUNGEON MASTER
---------------------
KNOCK ON DOOR, "MASTER,  FOLLOW ME", N, E, N, N, SET DIAL  TO 4, PRESS BUTTON,
S, OPEN CELL DOOR, S, "MASTER, GO N", "MASTER, SET DIAL TO 1 AND PUSH BUTTON",
UNLOCK BRONZE DOOR WITH KEY, OPEN BRONZE DOOR, S

(YOU WIN! WITH 7/7 POINTS)
