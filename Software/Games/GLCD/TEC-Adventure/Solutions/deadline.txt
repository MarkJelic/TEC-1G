AIM: MR. MARSHALL ROBNER HAS BEEN  MURDERED AT HIS  ESTATE, FIND WHO DID
IT AND ARREST THEM.

LIBRARY
-------
N, KNOCK  ON  DOOR, N, E, U, U, W, W, W,  W, N, GET  PENCIL, NOTEPAD AND
CALENDAR, TURN TO JULY 8, RUB NOTEPAD WITH PENCIL, S, E, E, E, E, S, S

DUMBAR'S BATHROOM
-----------------
OPEN CABINET, GET LOBLO, N, N, W, W, W, OPEN DOOR, S

GEORGE'S BEDROOM
----------------
SHOW CALENDAR  TO GEORGE, DROP CALENDAR AND PENCIL, N, E, E, E, D, D, W,
S, OPEN DOOR, S, E, E, SE

OUTSIDE
-------
GET LADDER, N, W, S, W, WAIT UNTIL 11:10 (PRESS Y  IF NEEDED), E, E, NW,
W, TALK TO MCNABB, WHAT  IS WRONG, SHOW ME  HOLES, FOLLOW MCNABB, INSERT
LADDER  INTO  HOLES, SEARCH  GROUND (AGAIN  UNTIL  YOU FIND  PORCELAIN),
ANALYSE FRAGMENT FOR LOBLO, N, W, S, N

LIVING ROOM
-----------
N, N, W, SHOW  NOTEPAD  TO  BAXTER, DROP  NOTEPAD, WAIT (UNTIL  SERGEANT
DUFFY ARRIVES), READ LAB REPORT, WAIT (UNTIL  WILL IS READ), E, E, U, U,
W, W, W, W, N, OPEN BALCONY DOOR

LIBRARY
-------
N, LOOK IN LIBRARY (UNTIL  YOU  SEE  GEORGE IN  SECRET  ROOM), READ  LAB
REPORT, S, WAIT 10 MINUTES, EXAMINE  SHELVES, PUSH BUTTON, LOOK IN SAFE,
GET PAPERS, READ PAPERS, W, S, E, E, E, E, D, D, W, W

LIVING ROOM
-----------
SHOW REPORT TO DUNBAR, WAIT (UNTIL MS. DUNBAR GLANCES AT BAXTER AND YOU)
E, W, WAIT (UNTIL  MS. DUNBAR  LEAVES), FOLLOW  DUNBAR (UNTIL SHE  DROPS
TICKET STUB), GET  STUB, SHOW  STUB TO  DUNBAR,  N, N, W,  SHOW STUB  TO
BAXTER. ARREST BAXTER AND DUNBAR

(YOU WIN!)
