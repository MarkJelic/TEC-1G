AIM: COLLECT EIGHT  ITEMS TO  BUILD A MACHINE THAT  DESTROYS THE LEATHER
     GODDESSES OF PHOBOS' ARMY

PHOBOS CELL
-----------
GET FOOD, GET ALL  EXCEPT TRAY, OPEN DOOR, S, OPEN  SOUTH DOOR, S, EXIT,
U, N, TURN LIGHT ON, DROP STOOL,  STAND ON STOOL, GET BASKET, D, STEP ON
CIRCLE

-------------

MACHINE, KNOCK, D, PUT FOOD IN CAGE, WAIT, WAIT, FUCK GORILLA, GET FOOD,

SWITCH [23], GET OFF SLAB, GET HOSE, PUT HOSE IN BASKET, STEP ON CIRCLE

VENUS JUNGLE
------------

MACHINE,  PUT JAR  IN  MACHINE,  CLOSE MACHINE,  TURN  MACHINE ON,  OPEN
MACHINE, GET JAR, DROP MACHINE AND MATCHBOOK, STEP ON CIRCLE

ATTACK SHIP
-----------

KILL THORBAST WITH  SWORD (REPEAT UNTIL HE DROPS HIS  SWORD), GET SWORD,
GIVE THORBAST  HIS SWORD [14],  KILL MONSTER,  UNTIE WOMAN, N  [18], PUT
PHOTO IN BASKET,  OPEN DOOR, E, S,  S, E, E, E,  E, E, E, E,  E, STEP ON
CIRCLE

MARS KING
---------
(RANDOM LOCATION DROP), (IF AT RUINED CASTLE  GO W, W, NW), (IF AT OASIS
GO W, NW,  W, W, NW), DROP  SWORD AND PAINTING, SHOW  PAINTING TO MOUSE,
GET MOUSE [21], PUT MOUSE IN BASKET, S, E, N, N, APPLY CREAM TO DAUGHTER
[21], DROP JAR,  GET ANGLE, PUT ANGLE  IN BASKET, S, S, E,  E, SE, STAIN
THE CIRCLE, DROP CAN, STEP ON CIRCLE

WINDOW, WAIT, WAIT [27], GET HEADLIGHT, PUT HEADLIGHT IN BASKET, GO DOWN
STAIRS, E, MOVE SOD, STEP ON CIRCLE

MARS SNOW
---------
DROP MAP AND TORCH, PUT PHONEBOOK IN BASKET, GET PIN, N, E, D [10], EXIT
BARGE, S, SE, DONATE COIN TO PENGUIN,  SE, N, WRAP BABY IN BLANKET, DROP
RAFT, EMPTY BASKET IN  RAFT, GET BABY, PUT BABY IN  BASKET, EXIT, S, PUT
BASKET ON STOOP, WAIT, WAIT, OPEN  DOOR, ENTER, GET BALLS [21], EXIT, N,
N, PUT BALLS IN RAFT, GET RAFT, S, NW, W, STEP ON CIRCLE

MARS FROG
---------
W, W, NW, APPLY BALM TO LIPS, PUT  BALLS IN EARS, PUT PIN ON NOSE, CLOSE
EYES, KISS FROG  [34], REMOVE PIN, REMOVE BALLS, REMOVE  BALM, DROP PIN,
GET BLENDER,  PUT BLENDER AND  BALLS IN  RAFT, W, N,  N, N, PUT  RAFT IN
WATER, ENTER  RAFT, WAIT, WAIT, WAIT,  WAIT, WAIT, WAIT, GRAB  DOCK, GET
RAFT, S, E, S,  BUY EXIT WITH COIN [16], SEARCH DUST,  N, OPEN TUBE, GET
CIRCLE, DROP TUBE AND CIRCLE, STAND ON CIRCLE
