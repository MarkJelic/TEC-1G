AIM: TO SEEK OUT AND DESTROY KRILL, EXPLORE HIS CASTLE AND LEARN IT SECRETS

GET FOOD AND DRINK AND ENTER CASTLE
-----------------------------------

NE, N, OPEN OVEN, GET BREAD AND JUG,  S, E, SE, NE, DRINK WATER [15], GET WATER,
SW, SE, SW, SW, S, NE, NE, E,  E, GNUSTO REZROV, LEARN REZROV, REZROV GATE [20],
E

GET MORE SCROLLS, DIE AND LIVE
------------------------------

LEARN FROTZ,  FROTZ BOOK [20], S,  S, E, S, OPEN  DOOR, N, REMOVE BLOCK,  E, GET
SCROLL, W, S, U, DROP ALL, E, GET LIGHTED PORTRAIT [25], GET SCROLL, W, GET ALL,
N, N, E, OZMOO ME, DROP ALL, E, WAIT, WAIT [35], D, W, GET ALL

GET MAGIC PROTECTION AND SLEEP
------------------------------

W, S, CUT ROPE [25], DROP DAGGER, OPEN  BOX, GET SCROLL, MELBOR ME, S, W, U, SIT
ON BED, SLEEP (REPEAT UNTIL YOU FALL ASLEEP)

GET DREAM, TAKE CONTROL OF TURTLE
---------------------------------

EAT BREAD  [10], GET UP, EXAMINE  BEDPOST, PUSH BUTTON [20],  GET SCROLL, GNUSTO
VAXUM, D,  E, E, E,  S, SE, LEARN NITFOL,  NITFOL TURTLE, EXEX  TURTLE, "TURTLE,
FOLLOW ME", NW,  N, E, U, "TURTLE,  SE, GET SCROLL, NW" [25],  GET SCROLL, DRINK
WATER, "TURTLE, THANKS"

FOLLOW RAT AND FIND HIDDEN SCROLL
---------------------------------

D, W,  N, N, N, N,  EXAMINE TRACK, REACH IN  HOLE [25], GNUSTO GONDAR,  N, LEARN
REZROV, REZROV GATE, N, GET SCROLL, E, LOOK UNDER LILY PAD

CRACK THE EGG AND FIX A SCROLL
------------------------------

W, S,  W, W, W,  W, W, U,  GET EGG, LEARN REZROV,  REZROV EGG [5],  GET SHREDDED
SCROLL, KREBF SHREDDED SCROLL [10], D, LEARN VAXUM, EAT BREAD

FIND ADVENTURER
---------------

E, (GO E,  E, E AND W,  W, W UNTIL YOU  SEE ADVENTURER IN MIRROR  AND HE WAITS),
ZIFMIA ADVENTURER [10], VAXUM ADVENTURER, SHOW EGG TO ADVENTURER, (GO EAST UNTIL
YOU GET TO A GUARDED DOOR), POINT AT DOOR [35], SAVE, N, (IF ADVENTURER PICKS UP
MAP OR  PENCIL GET  IT FROM HIM),  (WAIT UNTIL ADVENTURER  LEAVES THE  ROOM WITH
NOTHING, IF HE LEAVES WITH SOMETHING RESTORE GAME), DROP EGG, GET MAP AND PENCIL
(OR EITHER  ONE IF  YOU ALREADY HAVE  IT), (WHEN YOU  SEE THE  ADVENTURER AGAIN,
CLEESH ADVENTURER), DRINK WATER

SLEEP AND TRAP THE TERROR
-------------------------

S,  W, W,  W,  W,  W, W,  S,  S,  S, S,  U,  SIT ON  BED,  SLEEP  (IF YOU  DON'T
AUTOMATICALLY), GET UP, D, E,  D, D, S, E, NE, SE, CONNECT F  AND P, SW, SW, GET
SCROLL, ERASE B AND R, ERASE V AND M, NE, NW, NW, CONNECT B AND J, W [50]

DESTROY KRILL
-------------

U, U, E, E, N, N, N, EAT BREAD, E, E, LEARN VAXUM AND GONDAR, KULCAD STAIR [10],
IZYUK ME, E [10], GONDAR DRAGON, VAXUM BEING, GUNCHO KRILL [50]

(YOU WIN! WITH 400/400 POINTS)
